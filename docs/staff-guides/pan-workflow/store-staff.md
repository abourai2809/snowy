# Store Staff Pan Guide

Use this guide when working with gelato pans in the store.

## 1. Check In First

1. Sign in as **Store Staff**.
2. Tap **Attendance**.
3. Choose your store.
4. Upload selfie.
5. Tap **Check in**.

You must be checked in before store pan work.

## 2. Receive Pans From Lab

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Incoming pans**.
3. Confirm the store.
4. Check the flavour name.
5. Check the pan IDs listed under that flavour.
6. Tap **Accept all** only if every pan is correct.
7. If one pan is not received, tap **Missing** for only that pan.
8. If one pan is wrong or damaged, tap **Reject** for only that pan.
9. If one missing or rejected pan is later found, accept only that pan from **Rejected pans**.

![Store incoming pans](images/store-incoming-pans.png)

## 3. Move A Pan To Display

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Move to display**.
3. Choose the flavour.
4. Check **Recommended FIFO pan**.
5. Tap **Move pan**.

FIFO means use the oldest pan first. The app chooses the pan ID for you.

![Store FIFO recommendation](images/store-fifo-recommendation.png)

## 4. Bring Out A New Pan When Old Pan Is Too Low

1. In **Move to display**, choose the flavour.
2. Check the **Recommended FIFO pan**.
3. Under **Checkout**, choose **Too low, mark empty**.
4. Tap **Swap pan**.

The old pan will count as empty in the app.

![Store guided swap too low](images/store-guided-swap-too-low.png)

## 5. If Manager Says To Use A Different Pan

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Move to display**.
3. Choose the flavour.
4. Tap **Override FIFO**.
5. Choose the pan ID the manager told you to use.
6. Read the warning.
7. Tap **Move pan** or **Swap pan**.

Use **Override FIFO** only when Store Manager tells you.

## 6. Move Pan Outside Store For Event/B2B

Use this only when Store Manager or Admin tells you.

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Move outside store**.
3. Choose the flavour.
4. Choose the pan ID.
5. Check destination is **Event/B2B**.
6. Enter event or B2B name.
7. Tap **Move pan out**.

This pan will not be used for store FIFO after this.

## 7. Enter EOD Gelato Weight

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **EOD gelato weights**.
3. The app shows every pan that was in display today.
4. Enter the weight for each pan in kg.
5. Tap **Submit weight**.

If the pan is empty, enter **0**.
Do not enter a weight higher than the **Opening** weight shown by the app.

![Store EOD gelato weight](images/store-eod-gelato-weight.png)

## 8. Count Physical Empty Pans

Do this at beginning of day or end of day.

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Empty pan count**.
3. Enter how many empty pans you can see in the store.
4. Tap **Submit count**.

If the number does not match the app, the app will flag it for review.

![Store empty pan count](images/store-empty-pan-count.png)

## 9. Send Empty Pans Back To Lab

1. Tap **Store**.
2. Tap **Return empties**.
3. Enter the number of empty pans you are sending to lab.
4. Tap **Send to lab**.

Only enter pans that are physically leaving the store.

![Store return empty pans](images/store-return-empty-pans.png)

## Remember

- Always choose the recommended FIFO pan unless a manager tells you otherwise.
- In **Incoming pans**, do not reject the whole dispatch if only one pan is missing or wrong.
- If a pan has very little gelato, use **Too low, mark empty**.
- Move a pan outside store only for event/B2B and only when manager/Admin tells you.
- Enter weights in **kg**, not grams.
- At EOD, enter weight for each pan shown by the app.
- If something looks wrong, tell Store Manager.
